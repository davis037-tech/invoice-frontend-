Why invoice numbering matters

A consistent format makes invoices easy to track, easy to reference in emails, and — in many countries — is required for tax and accounting purposes. Keep it simple: a short prefix, an optional year, and a padded sequential number. Avoid reusing numbers, and avoid skipping around.

What every invoice template should include

  • Invoice number — a unique reference so both you and your client can track it (see our free invoice number generator if you're not sure how to format one)
  • Your business name and contact details
  • Client name and email
  • Issue date and due date — don't leave payment terms vague
  • An itemized list of what was delivered, with quantity and price
  • Subtotal, tax, and total — calculated, not guessed
  • How to pay — bank details or a payment link, clearly visible

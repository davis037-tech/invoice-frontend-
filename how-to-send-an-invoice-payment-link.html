How to Send an Invoice Payment Link

Getting paid shouldn't require a long back-and-forth with your client. An invoice payment link gives your client a simple way to access an invoice and view the payment information in one place.

What is an invoice payment link?

An invoice payment link is a shareable link connected to an invoice. Instead of sending a client a complicated file, you can send them a link they can open to view the invoice.

How to send an invoice payment link

1. Create your invoice

Add your client's information, the services or products you're charging for, and the relevant amounts.

2. Create your invoice link

Once the invoice is ready, generate its shareable link so your client can access the invoice.

3. Send the link to your client

Share the invoice link with your client through your usual communication channel, such as email or messaging.

4. Track the invoice

After sending the invoice, keep track of whether it has been paid. Ledger helps you keep track of invoices and identify unpaid or overdue invoices.

Why use an invoice payment link?

  • Your client has one place to view the invoice.
  • You can share the invoice directly with your client.
  • You can keep track of the invoice after sending it.
